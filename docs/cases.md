# Case interface and physical assumptions

JSON `schema: 1` cases resolve `board` and `output` relative to the case file.
All exported FEM quantities use metres, kilograms, seconds and pascals. Source
geometry, mesh spacing and mounting dimensions are specified in millimetres.

| Section | Interface |
|---|---|
| `material` | `young_pa`, `poisson`, `density_kg_m3`, `provenance` |
| `mounts` | `kind: holes` with `refs` and `outer_diameter_mm`, or `kind: cantilever` fixing minimum X |
| `components` | `enabled`, `mass_overrides_g` mapping fitted references to grams |
| `mesh` | `size_mm`, `mount_size_mm`, `layers`, `max_dofs` |
| `modal` | Increasing `batches`, `minimum_frequency_hz`, residual and orthogonality tolerances |
| `psd` | Increasing `frequency_hz`, matching nonnegative `density_g2_hz`, damping and convergence tolerances |
| `execution` | `backend`, `threads`, memory reserves and `time_budget_minutes` |
| `analysis` | `all`, `modal`, or `psd`; prerequisites are resolved automatically |

PSD values interpolate linearly between supplied breakpoints and are zero outside
the tabulated interval. Inputs are one-sided g²/Hz, using g=9.80665 m/s². X/Y/Z
cases are independent alternatives, not simultaneous or correlated axis loading.

Mounting has no implicit physical default. Every case must set `mounts.kind`.
For `holes`, also provide a nonempty list of unique `refs` and a positive finite
`outer_diameter_mm`. For `cantilever`, no hole references or contact diameter are
needed. Material defaults remain preliminary assumptions and can be overridden.

`study --case case.json` preserves the physical case and compares its in-plane
mesh spacing with a two-thirds refinement. It always runs modal and PSD stages;
`run case.json` honors the case's `analysis` selection. Study output defaults to
`results/studies/<board-stem>/<backend>/` and can be overridden with `--output`.

## Model

The homogeneous effective material defaults to E=24.37 GPa, ν=0.165 and
ρ=1944 kg/m³. This is the published preliminary PCB material used in an
[Ansys natural-frequency example](https://ansyshelp.ansys.com/public/Views/Secured/corp/v251/en/discovery_tech_show/Discovery/tech_show/nat_freq_analy_prnt_circ_board/c_nat_freq_mat_props.html),
not an identified Eurocircuits laminate. Thickness comes from the saved KiCad
board setting. Copper is not added again to this effective-board approximation.

Small drills, individual copper traces and solder joints are not resolved. Board
outline cutouts and selected mounting holes are retained. Quadratic tetrahedra
are generated by subdividing a two-layer extrusion. Physical group 2 identifies
finite fixed contacts; conversion must preserve constrained nodes.

Fitted component masses are distributed over board elements beneath their pad
attachment envelope. If no element centroid lies in a very small envelope, the
nearest elements receive the mass and the audit records this expansion. DNP
parts and mounting-hole footprints add no component mass. Override published or
measured masses with `mass_overrides_g` and describe their provenance in the case.
After case overrides, exact saved `MPN` matches use the manufacturer catalog in
`src/pcb_mechanical/masses.py`. It currently includes Harwin
[M80-5001642](https://www.harwin.com/products/M80-5001642) (1.238 g),
[M80-5002042](https://www.harwin.com/products/M80-5002042) (1.389 g), and
[M80-5T10442MC](https://www.harwin.com/products/M80-5T10442MC) (0.872 g).
These are nominal values; the manufacturer does not specify a mass tolerance.
Unmatched parts use pad-envelope area, package-class height and effective density,
with ±50% screening uncertainty. Missing manufacturer masses remain individually
flagged in the audit; the estimate never masquerades as a measured mass. No package stiffness or mass
offset is added. Density grouping preserves total assigned mass while limiting
SIF material count; the native assembled mass remains the numerical authority.

## Modal and PSD calculations

Elmer assembles K and M, exports them **before Dirichlet elimination or scaling**,
and solves Kφ=λMφ with λ=(2πf)². The pipeline checks residuals on free DOFs and
normalizes φᵀMφ=I. The CUDA bridge explicitly scales each inner solve because
Elmer's ARPACK dispatch bypasses its usual linear-system scaling. AMGX PCG runs
in double precision. One additional AMGX solve corrects the residual of the
original physical stiffness operator; residual accumulation uses extended CPU
precision to avoid cancellation on thin-plate bending problems. Participation uses the full mass matrix, including coupling to
constrained support nodes, under uniform base excitation.

For one axis, `q_j(f) = -Γ_j / (ω_j² - ω² + 2iζω_jω)`. The displacement transfer
function is the coherent sum of modal responses plus residual flexibility.
Absolute acceleration adds the unit base-acceleration term to `-ω² H_displacement`.
Residual stress and strain are recovered by the same native Elmer stress solver
from the static residual displacement shapes. Frequency integration forms modal
covariance matrices, retaining cross terms between modes. Field RMS values are
computed in bounded batches on NumPy or CuPy without allocating a full
node-by-frequency array.

Truncation compares the full retained basis against 75% of that basis with
consistent residual corrections. Insufficient response convergence increases the
mode count through the configured batches. The highest extracted frequency must
also exceed `minimum_frequency_hz` (3000 Hz by default). Participation fractions
are reported rather than presented as a substitute for response convergence.

The SIF and raw physical mass/stiffness matrices, modal binary files, numerical
NPZ interchange, logs and material audit remain available. Stress tensors use
Elmer's component order; scalar equivalent stress and fatigue are not inferred
from component RMS values. Reported field extrema are not a contact-edge stress
convergence claim.
